A Company Growing Faster Than Its Digital Presence

When auditing the original website at codemoly.com, the core problem became immediately apparent: CodeMoly had matured into an advanced software engineering partner building custom AI pipelines and industrial RMG ERP solutions, but its web presence still looked like a conventional SaaS template.
