DIAGNOSIS & STRATEGIC GAPS

A Company Growing Faster Than Its Digital Presence

When auditing the original website at codemoly.com, the core problem became immediately apparent: CodeMoly had matured into an advanced software engineering partner building custom AI pipelines and industrial RMG ERP solutions, but its web presence still looked like a conventional SaaS template.

01

Brand Gap

Heavy focus on basic n8n workflows obscured CodeMoly’s enterprise engineering and custom AI capabilities.

02

Passive UX

Relying on a standard YouTube video iframe and static cards without interactive WebGL physics or real-time depth.

03

Card Fatigue

Monotonous card grids with uniform layouts caused browsing fatigue and obscured key product value propositions.

04

Touch Friction

Rigid desktop-centric grids lacked tactile mobile ergonomics, requiring excessive vertical scrolling.
