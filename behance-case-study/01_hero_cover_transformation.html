01 / THE STARTING POINT Audit & Diagnosis

The Old Experience

CodeMoly had the right story — but the digital experience wasn't telling it strongly enough.

01

Repetitive Structure

Repeated content patterns reduced visual rhythm and made long-form exploration feel predictable.

02

Weak Hierarchy

Important messages, products and capabilities competed for attention instead of establishing clear visual priority.

03

Static Interaction

The experience relied heavily on static content, leaving little room for motion to support storytelling or discovery.

04

Limited Brand

The visual language didn't fully reflect CodeMoly's ambition across AI, technology, products and global capabilities.

THE DIAGNOSIS
Static • Repetitive • Information-heavy • Limited Interaction

The challenge wasn't simply to modernize the interface. It was to rethink how CodeMoly communicates its capabilities.

THE CHALLENGE

“The old experience communicated what CodeMoly does — but not what CodeMoly could become.”
